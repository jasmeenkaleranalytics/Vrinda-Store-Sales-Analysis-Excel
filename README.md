# Vrinda Store Sales Analysis Dashboard – Excel

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive sales analysis dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel.

The dashboard analyses sales and order data to identify patterns in monthly sales performance, customer demographics, geographical performance, sales channels and order fulfilment.

The project demonstrates the use of Excel for data analysis, PivotTables, PivotCharts, interactive slicers and business-focused data visualisation.

## Business Scenario

Vrinda Store wants to understand its sales performance and customer ordering patterns throughout 2022.

The dashboard provides an interactive way for management to explore sales performance across different months, customer groups, states, categories and sales channels.

## Business Questions

The dashboard was developed to investigate:

1. What is the total sales value and number of orders?
2. How does sales performance vary across months?
3. What proportion of sales comes from male and female customers?
4. What is the overall order fulfilment status?
5. Which states generate the highest sales?
6. How do sales vary across different age groups and genders?
7. Which sales channels generate the most sales?
8. How do sales performance and order patterns change when filtering by month or product category?

## Key Findings

### Monthly Sales Performance

The dashboard compares sales performance across all months of 2022, allowing monthly patterns and changes in sales to be identified.

### Gender Analysis

Women account for approximately 64% of sales, compared with 36% for men.

This indicates that female customers generated a larger proportion of sales within the dataset.

### Order Fulfilment

Approximately 92% of orders were delivered successfully.

The remaining orders were returned, cancelled or refunded.

### Geographical Analysis

Maharashtra recorded the highest sales among the top five states, followed by Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana and Tamil Nadu.

### Age and Gender Analysis

Sales are compared across Adult, Senior and Young customer groups, with a breakdown by gender.

This allows customer segments to be compared based on their contribution to sales.

### Sales Channel Analysis

Sales are compared across different channels including Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio, Meesho and Nalli.

Amazon represents the largest sales channel in the dataset at approximately 35%.

## Limitations

The analysis is based on the available 2022 sales dataset.

The dashboard identifies patterns within the data but does not establish the reasons behind changes in sales or customer behaviour.

Further analysis could incorporate additional information such as customer acquisition costs, marketing activity, product profitability and historical customer behaviour.
